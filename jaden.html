About Jaden

Five Seasons on the Deck

Jaden joined 805 LifeGuard in 2021 and has spent every season since safeguarding private events across Southern California — vigilant monitoring, water-safety protocols, and emergency response, delivered with the discretion our clients expect.

An American Red Cross certified Lifeguard with current CPR/AED and First Aid credentials, Jaden pairs rigorous training with an easy, personable presence. He is especially at home with younger guests — while parents appreciate a professional who watches the water with complete focus and coordinates seamlessly with hosts, planners, and vendors.

As a team lead, Jaden coordinates safety operations and emergency-response readiness across multi-guard events, carrying the 805 standard — excellence, discretion, peace of mind — to every engagement.
