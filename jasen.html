A Personal Introduction

Jasen Juarez

Director of Aquatic Operations — 805 LifeGuard

If you have watched an 805 LifeGuard team at work — a pool deck run with quiet precision, an event where the water simply felt safe — you have seen Jasen's work. As Director of Aquatic Operations, he is the standard-keeper between our lifeguards, our management, and the families we serve: every engagement briefed, every role clear, every detail held to the 805 standard.

Jasen brings eight years of certified lifeguarding, a supervisor's eye for a deck, and a teacher's patience to the role. He is also, simply, a waterman — the kind who can't stay out of the water for more than a few days, and never has been.

About Jasen

Made to Be in the Water

From a young age, Jasen's heart was drawn to the ocean. His dad would surf while he watched from the sand — until it was his turn to immerse himself in the waves. He hasn't really left since: ocean, river, or pool, he was made to be in the water.

The water shaped the résumé. Water polo and competitive swimming in high school. Eight years as a certified lifeguard — CPR certified throughout — guarding at Catalyst Kids, VillaSport Athletic Club and Spa, and Oaks Christian School. Supervisory experience at Catalyst Kids, and time as an assistant manager at Play It Again Sports in Ventura. An AA in social and behavioral studies, and a teaching career: Jasen serves as a middle-school Spanish teacher at Oaks Christian — the same patience and structure he brings to every swimmer he coaches.

At 805 LifeGuard, all of it converges: the waterman, the supervisor, and the teacher — responsible for the operational quality behind every engagement we take on.
