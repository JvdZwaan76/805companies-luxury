About Jasper

A Decade on the Deck

Jasper van der Zwaan came to private aquatics from the board. He dove competitively for Thousand Oaks High School, at a level that put him against the best in California — a discipline where the difference between a clean entry and a bad one is measured in degrees, and where every attempt is judged by people watching closely. What stayed with him was not the competition. It was the standard: the assumption that preparation is invisible when it is done properly, and conspicuous the moment it is not.

He founded 805 LifeGuard in 2016 with a straightforward premise. The professionalism he had known in competitive aquatics — the protocols, the certifications, the discipline of constant attention — existed almost nowhere in the private market. Families hosting a party at home, or booking lessons for a nervous five-year-old, were offered whoever happened to be available. He thought they deserved the same rigor he had trained under.

A decade on, that premise has held. Jasper maintains American Red Cross certification in Lifeguarding, CPR/AED for Professional Rescuers, and Water Safety Instruction, with advanced training in adaptive instruction for swimmers with special needs and physical challenges — work he considers among the most rewarding the profession offers. The firm is licensed for private-property service, bonded, and comprehensively insured, and every member of the team is background-checked before a first shift.

The practice is built around the Conejo Valley — Thousand Oaks, Westlake Village, Agoura Hills, and Newbury Park — and reaches across Southern California through a network of regional teams. What has not changed is the part clients tend to remember: the founder still teaches, still guards, and still reads every consultation request himself.

A Note from Jasper

Why I Still Get in the Water

"Every family deserves to enjoy their pool with complete peace of mind. My role isn't just about safety — it's about creating an environment where memories are made, confidence is built, and the joy of swimming can be experienced without worry."

"People sometimes ask why I still teach, when the firm could run without me on the deck. The honest answer is that I don't know how to hold a team to a standard I've stopped keeping myself. The moment I'm only reading reports, I've lost the thing that made this work worth building."

"It also happens to be the best part of the job. A child who was afraid of the deep end in June, swimming its length in August. A parent at their own party, actually enjoying it, because someone competent has the water. That is the whole business, in two pictures."

"If you're considering us, reach out directly. I read every consultation request myself."
