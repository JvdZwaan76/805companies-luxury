About Michael

Two Decades in the Water

Born in Washington, D.C., and raised in Costa Rica, Michael earned his BA in Exercise and Sport Science from the University of San Francisco, with an emphasis in Physiology and Applied Kinesiology and a minor in Spanish Language and Literature.

He began his teaching career in New York City, training alongside Olympic swimmers before joining Imagine Swimming, the city's premier swim school. Over six years there he became known for his work with young children — including children with special needs — and families regularly sought him out for private instruction at their residences and summer homes, where he managed every aspect of pool and ocean safety. His teaching was once demonstrated by Neil Patrick Harris on Late Night with Seth Meyers.

From 2010 he served as a head instructor, training staff across all age levels and working alongside notable coaches — from water polo and the Makos swim team to Parent & Me classes and synchronized swimming — while maintaining a full schedule across thirteen pools in the tri-state area. Summers were spent teaching surf on Long Island's East End: Sag Harbor, Amagansett, the Hamptons, and Montauk.

Michael eventually returned to California, coaching families in Santa Barbara and Montecito and serving as a swim coach at the Coral Casino Beach and Cabana Club before dedicating himself to exclusive house calls throughout Santa Barbara — high-quality instruction and peace of mind for children and adults alike.
